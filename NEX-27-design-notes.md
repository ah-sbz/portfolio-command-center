## Typography System

| Role | Font Stack | Size Range | Weight |
|------|-----------|------------|--------|
| Display / Headline | `Impact, Arial Black, sans-serif` | 160–420 px | Bold, condensed, all-caps |
| Body / Subtext | `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` | 56–80 px | Regular, sentence case |
| Letter-spacing | +10 to +30 px on headlines | — | Gives premium, intentional feel |

**Pro tip for final export:** Replace `Impact` with **Bebas Neue** or **Montserrat Bold** for a more modern, less "meme-y" look. These are free Google Fonts.

## Export Instructions

### Option A: SVG → Vector Editor → PNG (Recommended for precision)
1. Open any `.svg` in **Inkscape**, **Adobe Illustrator**, **Figma**, or **Canva**
2. Swap `Impact` for **Bebas Neue** or **Montserrat Bold** (optional but recommended)
3. Adjust colors if needed to match your selected shirt colors in Merch dashboard
4. Export PNG at **4500 × 5400 px**
5. Upload to Amazon Merch

### Option B: ChatGPT Plus / DALL-E (Fast but less precise on typography)
1. Copy the prompt from `NEX-27-prompt-log.md`
2. Generate in ChatGPT Plus image mode
3. Download and upscale/crop to 4500 × 5400 px using a free tool (e.g., letsenhance.io, imgupscaler.com)
4. Upload to Amazon Merch

### Option C: Browser Preview → Screenshot (Quick test)
1. Open any `.svg` in Chrome/Firefox
2. Use DevTools to set viewport to 4500 × 5400
3. Screenshot at 1× scale (useful for quick visual validation)
