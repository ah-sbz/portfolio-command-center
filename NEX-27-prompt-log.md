## Tips for Better DALL-E Typography Results

1. **Ask for "no illustrations, no clip art, no decorations"** — DALL-E loves to add random graphics
2. **Specify "pure typography" or "text only"** to reduce visual noise
3. **Give exact hex codes** for colors to keep the palette cohesive
4. **If text is misspelled,** ask for a regeneration with: *"Spell [WORD] exactly: [letter-by-letter if needed]"*
5. **For fastest iteration,** generate at lower resolution first, pick the best concept, then ask for a high-resolution version
6. **DALL-E is weaker at long phrases** — keep each text block to 2–6 words for best legibility
